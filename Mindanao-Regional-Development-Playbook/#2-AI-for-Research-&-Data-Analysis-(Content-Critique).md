## Executive Overview

AI discovery and summarization tools offer rapid synthesis of academic literature, but they present significant operational risks when evaluating localized emerging fintech trends in the Philippines. Because regional data on digital micro-lending apps (e.g., Shopee SpayLater, LazPayLater, Billease) is scarce in global training datasets, AI engines frequently hallucinate regional statistics, invent institutional reports, or falsely extrapolate Western consumer behavior patterns onto the socio-economic realities of students in the Davao Region.

This log functions as a rigorous manual audit. It maps automated AI summaries against verified primary sources—including the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP), National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA) XI, and localized peer-reviewed literature—ensuring empirical integrity before these insights transition into formal policy briefs.

## 2. Critical Reflection on Tool Limitations

While AI platforms excel at consolidating dense, multi-page theoretical concepts into scannable thematic summaries, their structural limitations present distinct hazards for localized economic research:

1. **The "Local Data Desert" Vulnerability:** AI models are heavily trained on Western consumer finance data (e.g., studies on Klarna or Afterpay usage among US/EU college students). When forced to generate insights on Mindanao-specific demographics, the model seamlessly blends Western spending metrics with local Philippine terminology (like "Barangay" or "Local LGU"), creating highly believable but completely unempirical conclusions.
2. **Deceptive Source Anchoring:** As seen in the audit matrix, the AI engine successfully anchored itself to real, authoritative organizations like NEDA XI and the BSP. However, it completely fabricated the *contents* of the reports themselves to satisfy the user's specific prompt requirements. This proves that an AI's professional tone and institutional naming are zero guarantee of factual truth.
3. **Inability to Parse Contextual Nuance:** The AI failed to capture the unique financial ecosystem of a Davao City college student—whose disposable income is often tied to fixed allowances or informal stipends rather than structured salaries, making the "financial stress" dynamics of deferred payments radically different from an employed Western consumer.

## 3. Established Policy / Research Safeguards

To prevent unverified AI outputs from compromising our university research center's academic standing, the following operational safeguards are now mandatory for all team members:

* **The Primary Source Anchor Rule:** No statistic or citation generated by an AI summarizer may be typed into a research draft unless the primary PDF file or official institutional database URL has been downloaded, opened, and manually cross-checked by a human researcher.
* **Prohibition of Open-Ended Synthesis:** Researchers are prohibited from prompting AI tools with generalized queries such as *"Tell me about student debt in Davao."* Instead, prompts must include explicit source limitations, such as: 
  > *"Summarize the attached 2023 BSP Financial Inclusion Survey PDF. Limit your findings strictly to the data explicitly written in Chapters 3 and 4. Do not infer external regional trends."*
* **Mandatory Fact-Checking Appendix:** Every policy brief, thesis milestone, or whitepaper originating from this unit must append an updated version of this *Literature Verification Log*, detailing exactly which automated claims were audited, flagged, or corrected.
